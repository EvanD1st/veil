# Judge walkthrough and recording script

The deployed digital-delivery baseline is cb5ab9f; the new Genesis automatic-edition implementation is a candidate until separately approved. See [the self-service judging status and wallet gate](SELF_SERVICE_JUDGING.md). Managed-wallet activation/funding cannot currently complete, so the full requested self-service acceptance flow remains blocked. Current live URL: https://ve-il.duckdns.org. Register/login with a username and password; use separate real seller and bidder accounts, never share passwords or auction keys. The operator must provision coverage for the seller's own receiver before digital publishing.

1. **Discover (20 s).** Show Explore and real category filters, the clearly labelled sample concepts and the testnet notice. Existing published legacy results are available for a quick inspection: open a real result and independently verify its highest-bid proof. Legacy records have no retroactive file entitlement.
2. **Publish (40 s).** As the eligible seller, upload a sanitized preview and protected PNG/JPEG or binary STL original. State provenance, license, format/size and any seller-declared edition. Download the private auction-key backup off camera. Explain frozen terms and that this is not an NFT or copyright certificate.
3. **Bid (20 s).** In an isolated ordinary buyer session, open the listing and seal one bid. Show the recorded commitment/acceptance, not private openings. The seller cannot bid on their own lot. Use a short real deadline, ordinary clocks and a future drand round; cut the recording while waiting without implying instant unlock.
4. **Reveal and verify (40 s plus proving).** Seller restores the backup, opens every accepted envelope, generates/publishes the proof. Show cold asset progress or disclose cached assets. In a third account independently verify the result. Only the winning opening is published; the seller can see losing bids after reveal.
5. **Pay once (separate approval).** Winner opens the NEW canonical invoice. Pause before any transfer; obtain approval for exact network/address/amount/memo/fee. After an authorized actual shielded transfer, identify its exact incoming output, let the trusted scanner verify active chain/confirmations, and show automatic signed delivery. Never reuse the previously paid legacy invoice. Do not expose memo, private keys, cookies or unrelated payment metadata in a public recording.
6. **Receive (20 s).** Winner's My purchases shows earned access and frozen license. Download and compare the exact original privately. Outsider/losing account gets denied; logging out also denies access. Explain that expiry of a status report does not remove an earned grant, and explicit invalidation can suspend future downloads but cannot recall a file.

A fixture demonstration must be labelled **synthetic payment observation — no live transfer** throughout step 5. Native CI exercises this full application flow using actual files/accounts, live future drand/proofs and a synthetic signed confirmation. It is automated evidence, not a hosted payment. A previously verified legacy payment is separate historical evidence. The new hosted payment/download, new physical-phone upload/download checks, copyright/provenance verification and independent security audit remain uncompleted until recorded.

Phone checklist on the actual Redmi K30 Pro/Chrome: upload preview/original, cancel/retry, review license/type/size, bid, back up/restore safely, run proof with cold/cached timings, access My purchases and download, rotate/keyboard/no sideways scrolling, logout/outsider denial. Do not send secrets or screenshots containing keys/memos. Report Android/Chrome versions and measured timings when available. Browser viewport emulation alone is not physical-device evidence.
